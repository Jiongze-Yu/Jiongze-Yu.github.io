## About Me {#about}

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at [Texas A&M University](https://engineering.tamu.edu/cse/), advised by [Prof. Zhengzhong Tu](https://vztu.github.io/). Before that, I received my M.S. degree from [Sun Yat-sen University](https://www.sysu.edu.cn/) in 2025, advised by [Prof. Ronghui Zhang](https://ise.sysu.edu.cn/teacher/ZhangRonghui) and [Prof. Junzhou Chen](https://ise.sysu.edu.cn/teacher/ChenJunzhou), and my B.S. degree from [Southwest Jiaotong University](https://www.swjtu.edu.cn/) in 2022.

My research centers on **high-resolution, high-quality, and efficient** video generation, editing, and restoration. Beyond pixels, I am exploring how video generative models can serve as **world models** and power **agentic systems**, and how they can be brought to real-world **AI+X** applications. Along the way, I have been fortunate to collaborate with researchers at **Google** and **YouTube**.

## Research Interests {#interests}

- **Video Generation & World Models:** real-time, interactive and long video generation
- **Low-Level Computer Vision:** video super-resolution, image/video restoration
- **Video Editing:** instruction-based editing, visual effects, scene text editing
- **Multimodal LLMs & AI+X:** MLLM-based evaluation, agentic systems, and real-world applications
